04 / Listing Lifecycle

How a business goes live

Every listing moves through a moderated state machine before customers can see it.

State 1
Draft
→
State 2
Pending Review
→
State 3
Active
→
State 4
Expired / Suspended
Draft → Pending Review Owner submits the listing. Goes into an admin moderation queue to prevent spam, fake businesses, or inappropriate content.
Pending → Active Admin approves. For commercial categories, an active paid subscription is required. Churches & funeral homes skip payment — they go straight to Active.
Active → Expired Subscription lapses (Stripe webhook fires). Listing is hidden from search. Owner can reactivate by renewing — no data is lost.
categories.is_free_category = TRUE — this DB flag bypasses subscription enforcement entirely. Churches and funeral homes are always free and never expire due to non-payment.

06 / Security & Trust

Keeping the platform safe

Trust is the product — especially for home-service and community categories.

⭐

Review Integrity

One review per customer per business — enforced by a unique DB constraint. Reviews can be flagged for moderation via is_flagged. Owner replies are stored alongside the original review.

✓

Business Verification

is_verified badge — manually confirmed phone, address, and business license. Especially important for home-service trades and churches where scams are a concern.

🛡

Rate Limiting

Applied on review submission, messaging, and booking requests to prevent spam and abuse. Protects both the platform and business owners from fake interactions.

🔒

PII Protection

Customer phone and email are only visible to a business after a booking or message is initiated — never exposed in public search results or listing pages.
